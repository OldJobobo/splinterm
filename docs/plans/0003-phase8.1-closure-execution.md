## Execution policy

Each slice must:

1. preserve daemon ownership, authentication, scope checks, and all protocol bounds;
2. keep Foot as the observable authority unless ADR 0004 records a reviewed divergence;
3. avoid per-character offsets, best-fit image translation, broad tolerances, and silent reference regeneration;
4. include focused tests and update the relevant spike/ADR evidence;
5. pass `cargo fmt --all --check`, strict workspace Clippy, workspace tests, and `git diff --check`; and
6. remain independently reviewable before the next dependent slice starts.

### Source-first parity policy

Pinned Foot source is the normative specification for deterministic renderer
formulas, ordering, and state transitions. Port those contracts directly and
lock them with exact source-derived unit vectors before adding graphical tests.
Graphical Foot runs are reserved for integration behavior that source inspection
cannot prove: final cross-implementation composition, Wayland/output scaling,
focus presentation, environment/font resolution, and compositor residuals.

This policy reduces redundant Wayland matrices, not parity coverage:

- the full face/size/scale/corpus coverage remains in headless fcft, isolated
  FreeType, production-cache, metrics, placement, fallback, and cache-identity
  comparisons;
- exact grayscale and declared fixture-specific color policies remain unchanged;
- full/dirty/scroll/cache equivalence remains byte-exact and internal;
- graphical matrices use boundary and representative integration cases rather
  than replaying every source-derived decoration formula at every scale; and
- unsupported or intentional divergences must still be recorded in ADR 0004.
